# The story, as a state machine.
#
# A quest is a list of stages. Stage k is entered when its condition holds, and
# entering it writes its journal line and may raise a flag. Only the next stage
# is ever tested, so a quest cannot skip ahead.
#
#   quest ID              the name a data file calls it by
#     name TEXT           what the journal calls it
#     stage               one step; they are entered in order
#       when COND         what has to be true to enter it
#       journal TEXT      what the journal says once it is
#       sets FLAG         a flag raised on entering, for other quests to see
#       worth N           what entering it teaches the party, in experience
#       ends              entering it is the end of the story
#
# The conditions are things the world already knows how to answer:
#
#   when knows WORD       the player has learned a word - from anybody
#   when has ITEM N       the player is carrying N of a thing
#   when flag NAME        some other stage raised it
#   when slain N          N have fallen to the party
#   when party N          N walk with the Avatar
#   when at PLACE         the Avatar is standing in that map - the name of
#                         its file without the extension, so `vale` is the
#                         same place whichever form of it is on disk
#   when at PLACE X Y R   ...and within R tiles of that spot in it
#
# A `when` with nothing after it follows the stage before it on the same turn,
# which is how a stage that only writes a line is written. The *first* stage of
# a quest may not do that - it would begin the moment a game did, which is a
# quest nobody was given.
#
# The line on the stage marked `ends` is what the game says at the end, so the
# closing words of the story are a line in this file like every other line.

quest CARAVAN
  name The Missing Caravan
  stage
    when knows caravan
    journal The market stall is bare: a caravan out of the north is three weeks late.
    sets heard_of_caravan
    worth 20
  stage
    when knows road
    journal The gate was watched for a fortnight and nothing came down the road.
  stage
    when knows stones
    journal The north road runs past standing stones older than the vale itself.
  stage
    when knows light
    journal A cold light has been seen among the stones, since the week it failed.
    sets knows_the_light
    worth 30
  stage
    when knows north
    journal Nobody has come down that road since. Somebody must go and look.
    sets caravan_understood
    worth 40
  stage
    when at stones
    journal The stones stand in a ring, and the grass inside them is beaten flat.
    sets stood_among_the_stones
    worth 40
  stage
    when at stones 24 22 6
    journal A man waits in the middle of the ring, and he is not surprised to see thee.
  stage
    when has SILVER 3
    journal Rugar is down, and the caravan's silver is in thy pack. Take it home.
    sets caravan_avenged
    worth 120
  stage
    when at vale
    journal Thou art home with it. Iolo is at his stall; ask him of the SILVER.
  stage
    when flag silver_returned
    journal The caravan's silver is on Iolo's counter, the road north is open, and the vale owes thee a debt it will be telling for years.
    sets caravan_returned
    worth 200
    ends

quest COMPANY
  name Not Alone
  stage
    when party 1
    journal Somebody walks with thee. The north road is not one to walk alone.
    sets have_company
    worth 25
  stage
    when party 2
    journal Two walk with thee. Whatever waits among the stones may reconsider.
    sets well_companied
    worth 25

quest BRIGANDS
  name The Hills Are Not Safe
  stage
    when slain 1
    journal There are brigands in the hills, and they did not come from nowhere.
  stage
    when slain 4
    journal Four of them now, and thickest of all along the north road.
    sets hills_thinned
    worth 40
  stage
    when flag caravan_understood
    journal It fits: the road was stopped, and these are who stopped it.
    sets story_understood
    worth 60
