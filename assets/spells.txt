# The book of spells.
#
# A spell is a phrase, not an entry in a list. You can cast it when you know the
# runes it is made of, and runes are learned the way every other word in this
# game is learned: somebody tells you one. Ask Nystul.
#
#   rune WORD MEANING       declare a rune; must come before any spell using it
#   spell WORD [WORD...]    a spell, named by the phrase that casts it
#     name TEXT             what it is called in the book
#     circle N              how deep a mage it wants, 1 to 8
#     costs ITEM N [...]    reagents spent on every casting
#     effect WHAT [N] [turns N] [reach N]
#     say TEXT              what it reads like when it works
#
# Six effects, and each one has to be visible in the world - a spell whose
# result nobody can see is a control that does nothing. What the three numbers
# mean is the effect's own business, and any it does not use is left out:
#
#   light   N is how far it reaches        turns is how long it holds
#   heal    N is health returned
#   harm    N is damage                                     reach is how far
#   sleep   N is how many go down          turns is how long, reach is how far
#   ward    N is guard added               turns is how long
#   travel                                                  reach is how far

rune AN    negate
rune IN    create
rune LOR   light
rune MANI  life
rune VAS   great
rune FLAM  flame
rune NOX   poison
rune ZU    sleep
rune SANCT protect
rune POR   movement

spell IN LOR
  name Light
  circle 1
  costs ash 1
  effect light 5 turns 60
  say A cold light gathers about thee and holds.

spell VAS IN LOR
  name Great Light
  circle 3
  costs ash 2 bloodmoss 1
  effect light 7 turns 150
  say The dark is driven back a long way, and stays back.

spell MANI
  name Heal
  circle 1
  costs ginseng 1
  effect heal 10
  say Thy hurts close over.

spell VAS MANI
  name Great Heal
  circle 3
  costs ginseng 2 bloodmoss 1
  effect heal 25
  say Warmth runs through thee and the pain is gone.

spell IN FLAM
  name Fire Bolt
  circle 2
  costs ash 1 bloodmoss 1
  effect harm 8 reach 5
  say A bolt of flame leaps from thy hand.

spell VAS IN FLAM
  name Great Fire
  circle 4
  costs ash 2 bloodmoss 1 nightshade 1
  effect harm 18 reach 6
  say Fire roars out and does not stop where thou meant it to.

spell IN NOX
  name Poison Bolt
  circle 2
  costs nightshade 1
  effect harm 5 reach 4
  say A green mist gathers and darts away from thee.

spell AN NOX
  name Cure
  circle 2
  costs ginseng 1 nightshade 1
  effect heal 6
  say The sickness goes out of thee.

spell IN ZU
  name Sleep
  circle 2
  costs nightshade 1
  effect sleep 1 turns 8 reach 4
  say Its eyes close, and it goes down where it stood.

spell VAS ZU
  name Great Sleep
  circle 4
  costs nightshade 2 ginseng 1
  effect sleep 4 turns 6 reach 5
  say The word goes out flat across the ground, and what hears it lies down.

spell IN SANCT
  name Ward
  circle 2
  costs ginseng 1 bloodmoss 1
  effect ward 4 turns 40
  say The air about thee thickens and will not be hurried.

spell VAS POR
  name Blink
  circle 3
  costs bloodmoss 2
  effect travel reach 6
  say Thou art elsewhere, and the way thou wert facing.
