# The book of what people say.
#
# Read by the game, written by hand for now and by the editor later. Nothing in
# here is compiled in: adding a person or a topic is an edit to this file.
#
#   person NAME        starts a block: everything about one person
#   art    ID          which sprite they wear - and that the world places them
#   home   PLACE       the region they live in; a map with one gets them
#   at     HH DX DY    at this hour, be this far from that place's centre
#   greet  TEXT        what they say when you walk up to them
#   topic  WORD [WORD] a thing they will talk about, and its synonyms
#     say  TEXT        a line of the answer; up to three
#     teach WORD       asking this hands over a word you did not have
#     joins            asking this takes them with thee
#     wants ITEM N     only askable while thou hast N of it, and asking gives
#                      them away - which is how a thing is handed to somebody
#     gives ITEM N     asking hands N of it over, but only if thou hast none
#                      already - so a topic asked twice is not a free purse
#     trade            makes a `gives` a purchase and a `wants` a sale. The
#                      price is the thing's own value in the item table, and a
#                      merchant buys at half what he sells for. A topic that
#                      sells is only offered when thou canst pay for it.
#     raises FLAG      asking this settles something the story is watching for
#   bye    TEXT        what they say as you leave
#
# The offsets in `at` are from the centre of their `home`, not tiles on the map:
# the generator puts the town somewhere different every seed, so an absolute
# position would land these people in a field. A map authored in the editor
# records absolute positions instead, and a map that names somebody itself keeps
# its own version of them rather than the book's.
#
# The first eight live in Britain, which is the region the generator's town is
# called and the region the vale ships with - so those eight are in both. The
# four after them live in Wyndle, down the road east, and appear only in a map
# that has a region of that name. A map with neither has never heard of any of
# them.
#
# Everyone begins knowing NAME and JOB and nothing else. Every other word in
# this file has to be taught by some topic, or nobody can ever ask it - which is
# also how a rumour crosses a town: Iolo teaches CARAVAN, and it is Shamino and
# Nell who know what became of it.

person Iolo
art MERCHANT
home Britain
at 06 -6 -4
at 12 0 -6
at 18 6 -4
at 22 -6 2
greet Hail, Avatar! The market opens at dawn, and closes when I say.
topic name
  say Iolo, and my father Iolo before me. The stall is older than either.
topic job market stall
  say I buy and I sell, and lately I do more of the one than the other.
  say BREAD I have, and a TORCH or two. I will pay for NIGHTSHADE if thou hast any.
  say My shelves are otherwise bare. Ask of the CARAVAN and thou wilt know why.
  teach caravan
  teach bread
  teach torch
  teach nightshade
topic caravan
  say Three weeks late out of the north, and not a word from it since.
  say Shamino watched the road that fortnight. Ask HIM what he saw.
  say And take my father's hammer. Thou wilt not walk that road empty-handed.
  teach road
  gives HAMMER 1
topic bread
  say Bread, then. Fresh this morning, and cheap enough at the price.
  gives BREAD 2
  trade
topic torch
  say A torch. Thou wilt want two if thou art going anywhere dark.
  gives TORCH 2
  trade
topic nightshade
  say Nightshade? Nystul pays me for that, so I will pay thee.
  wants NIGHTSHADE 1
  trade
topic silver
  say Silver? Out of the north, with the caravan's mark still on it?
  say Then it is true, and thou hast walked further than any of us dared.
  say Give it here, Avatar. The vale is in thy debt and the stall is open.
  wants SILVER 3
  raises silver_returned
bye Come back when thou hast coin, Avatar.

  teach nox

  say None of them are glad to be seen. Ask Gwenno of the NORTH.
  teach north
