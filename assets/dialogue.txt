# The book of what people say.
#
# Read by the game, written by hand for now and by the editor later. Nothing in
# here is compiled in: adding a person or a topic is an edit to this file.
#
#   person NAME        starts a block: everything about one person
#   art    ID          which sprite they wear - and that the world places them
#   home   PLACE       the region they live in; a map with one gets them
#   at     HH DX DY    at this hour, be this far from that place's centre
#   greet  TEXT        what they say when you walk up to them
#   topic  WORD [WORD] a thing they will talk about, and its synonyms
#     say  TEXT        a line of the answer; up to three
#     teach WORD       asking this hands over a word you did not have
#     joins            asking this takes them with thee
#   bye    TEXT        what they say as you leave
#
# The offsets in `at` are from the centre of their `home`, not tiles on the map:
# the generator puts the town somewhere different every seed, so an absolute
# position would land these people in a field. A map authored in the editor
# records absolute positions instead, and a map that names somebody itself keeps
# its own version of them rather than the book's.
#
# Everyone here lives in Britain, which is the region the generator's town is
# called and the region the vale ships with - so these eight are in both. A map
# with no region of that name has never heard of them.
#
# Everyone begins knowing NAME and JOB and nothing else. Every other word in
# this file has to be taught by some topic, or nobody can ever ask it - which is
# also how a rumour crosses a town: Iolo teaches CARAVAN, and it is Shamino and
# Nell who know what became of it.

greet Hail, Avatar! The market opens at dawn, and closes when I say.
topic name
  say Iolo, and my father Iolo before me. The stall is older than either.
topic job market stall
  say I buy and I sell, and lately I do more of the one than the other.
  say My shelves are bare. Ask of the CARAVAN and thou wilt know why.
  teach caravan
topic caravan
  say Three weeks late out of the north, and not a word from it since.
  say Shamino watched the road that fortnight. Ask HIM what he saw.
  teach road
bye Come back when thou hast coin, Avatar.

  say Nystul, of the tower that was, before the roof went.

  say I read what is written above, and write down what little I understand.

  say A spell is a phrase, not a trick. Learn the words and the spells follow.
  say MANI is life, and IN is to make. Say them and see.
  teach mani
topic mani
  say Life. Alone it closes a wound; after VAS it closes a great deal more.
  teach in
topic in
  say To make. IN LOR makes light, and IN FLAM makes something less friendly.
  teach lor
topic lor
  say Light. Thou hast the two thou needest for it now.
  say Ask of VAS if thou wouldst have more of anything.
  teach vas
topic vas
  say Great. It goes before another word and makes it more than it was.
  say FLAM and NOX I will teach thee only if thou hast cause. Hast thou?
  teach flam
topic flam
  say Flame. Have a care where thou pointest it.
  teach nox
topic nox
  say Poison. AN NOX undoes it, which is the more useful of the two.
  teach an
topic an
  say To undo. It is the first word a careful mage learns and the last one
  say a careless one wishes he had.
topic light
  say A cold light is not a fire and not a lamp. That leaves few things.
  say None of them are glad to be seen. Ask Gwenno of the NORTH.
  teach north
topic stones
  say Older than the vale, and older than whatever now walks among them.
bye Look up more often, Avatar.
