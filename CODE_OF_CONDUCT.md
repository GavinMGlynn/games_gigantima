# Code of conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in this
project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size,
visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity
and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive and healthy community.

## Our standards

Behaviour that helps:

- Being kind to other people, and assuming they meant well
- Respecting differing opinions, viewpoints and experiences
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback
- Taking responsibility, apologising to those affected by our mistakes, and
  learning from them
- Caring about what is best for the community as a whole, not only for us

Behaviour that will not be tolerated:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information, such as a physical or email address,
  without their explicit permission
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting

## Scope

This applies within all project spaces — the repository, its issues, its pull
requests and its discussions — and also when an individual is officially
representing the project in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be
reported to the maintainer by opening an issue, or privately through GitHub at
<https://github.com/GavinMGlynn>. All complaints will be reviewed and
investigated promptly and fairly, and the reporter's privacy and security will
be respected.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this code in good faith may face
temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other members of the
project's leadership.

### What happens

1. **A quiet word.** For unprofessional or unwelcome behaviour: a private
   written warning saying what was wrong and why, and a request for an apology
   where one is due.
2. **A warning.** For a single serious incident, or a repeated one: a warning
   with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with those
   involved for a stated period.
3. **A temporary ban.** For a serious violation, including sustained
   inappropriate behaviour: no public or private interaction with the community
   for a stated period.
4. **A permanent ban.** For a pattern of violation, harassment of an individual,
   or aggression toward or disparagement of groups of people.
